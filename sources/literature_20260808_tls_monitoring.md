# Literature Source Audit

Retrieved and checked on 2026-08-08 for the exploratory AHCLM manuscript. The project-local research lookup client could not be used because neither `PARALLEL_API_KEY` nor `OPENROUTER_API_KEY` was configured. The manuscript therefore cites the primary standards and the original Tranco publication directly.

1. D. Cooper et al. *Internet X.509 Public Key Infrastructure Certificate and Certificate Revocation List (CRL) Profile*, RFC 5280, May 2008. DOI: https://doi.org/10.17487/RFC5280. Primary source: https://www.rfc-editor.org/rfc/rfc5280.html
2. R. Barnes, J. Hoffman-Andrews, D. McCarney, and J. Kasten. *Automatic Certificate Management Environment (ACME)*, RFC 8555, March 2019. DOI: https://doi.org/10.17487/RFC8555. Primary source: https://www.rfc-editor.org/rfc/rfc8555.html
3. A. Gable. *ACME Renewal Information (ARI) Extension*, RFC 9773, June 2025. DOI: https://doi.org/10.17487/RFC9773. Primary source: https://www.rfc-editor.org/rfc/rfc9773.html
4. V. Le Pochat, T. Van Goethem, S. Tajalizadehkhoob, M. Korczynski, and W. Joosen. *Tranco: A Research-Oriented Top Sites Ranking Hardened Against Manipulation*. NDSS Symposium, 2019. DOI: https://doi.org/10.14722/ndss.2019.23386. Primary publication record: https://repository.tudelft.nl/file/File_403db0ca-1378-4c15-be36-979d0625bd63
5. B. Laurie, E. Messeri, and R. Stradling. *Certificate Transparency Version 2.0*, RFC 9162, December 2021. DOI: https://doi.org/10.17487/RFC9162. Primary source: https://www.rfc-editor.org/rfc/rfc9162.html

The paper makes no numerical claim from these sources. They provide protocol and sampling context; all reported measurements come from the frozen local snapshot cited in the reproducibility materials.
